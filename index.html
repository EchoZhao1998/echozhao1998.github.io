Echo Zhao

data science · research · accessibility

I am a Master's student in Data Science at Monash University Malaysia, exploring the intersection of data, design, and human systems. Through research and writing, I aim to make complex information easier to understand and use — particularly in the areas of technology, work, and inclusion.

As a late-deafened person living with neurofibromatosis type 2 (NF2), I bring lived experience to this work. I am especially drawn to projects that bridge the gap between what disabled people need and what existing systems assume they need.
